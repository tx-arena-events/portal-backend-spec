# C2-M1 Sign-in Services

**Version:** dropped, as of 2026-10-28
**Capability:** C2 Identity and Access
**Kind:** dropped with OSP 2.0

With OSP 2.0, the Portal IAM of an OSP has exactly one identity provider: the EBW-Connector of the installation. Identity providers per PSP are dropped.

**Where the tasks went:** The identity provider is fixed configuration in the Portal IAM (C2-M5). C2-M2 Users and App Access manages the accounts of people and their binding to a company.
